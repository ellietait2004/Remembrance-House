Frequently asked questions

Is this a physical product?

No. Remembrance House sells digital editable templates. Nothing is posted to you.

Do you personalise the template for me?

The templates are designed for customers to edit themselves. You can replace the sample wording and images with your own content.

Can I change the photographs and wording?

Yes. The purpose of the template is to allow you to personalise the details for your loved one.

How do I print my finished Order of Service?

After editing, download the finished design and print it at home or use a professional printing service.

Can I use the template more than once?

Your final licence terms should clearly explain permitted personal use, copying and resale.

What if I need help?

If you have questions about accessing, editing or downloading your template, please get in touch.

About Remembrance House

Remembrance House offers thoughtfully designed digital funeral stationery to help families create a beautiful and personal tribute.

Our editable templates are made to be simple to use, giving you the freedom to add your own photographs, words and memories while keeping a polished, elegant design.

We know these details are being created during an important and emotional time. Our aim is to offer calm, tasteful templates that make the process feel a little simpler.
